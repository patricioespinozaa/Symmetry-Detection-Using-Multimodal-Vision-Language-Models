You are given ONE image of a 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant axis of rotational symmetry.

Your task is to identify the two poles of the rotation axis: the topmost and bottommost points of the object where the symmetry axis exits the surface.

Identify:
- the TOP POLE: the topmost visible surface point of the object, where the axis exits at the TOP.
- the BOTTOM POLE: the bottommost visible surface point of the object, where the axis exits at the BOTTOM.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST lie ON the symmetry axis (at the center of the object's cross-section, not on the side silhouette).
- The TOP POLE MUST have a smaller Y value than the BOTTOM POLE.
- The two points MUST be as far apart vertically as possible.
- Both points MUST lie on the visible object surface.
- Do NOT place points on the lateral silhouette edges — they must be at the top and bottom centers.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same location.

Coordinates are relative to the image's own width/height, on a 0-1000 scale with (0, 0) at the top-left corner.

Output ONLY a JSON list with exactly two entries, in this exact format:

[
  {"label": "top", "point_2d": [x, y]},
  {"label": "bottom", "point_2d": [x, y]}
]

Do not include any text before or after the JSON list.
