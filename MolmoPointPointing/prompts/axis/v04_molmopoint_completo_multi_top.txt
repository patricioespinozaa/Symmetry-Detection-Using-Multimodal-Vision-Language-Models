You are given multiple views of the SAME 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant axis of rotational symmetry.

For each image, identify the two poles of the global rotation axis: the topmost and bottommost points where the axis exits the object's surface.

For each image, locate where the global axis exits at the top and bottom of the object.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST lie ON the rotation axis (at the center of the object, not on the lateral silhouette).
- Use the SAME global axis consistently across all views.
- The top pole MUST be above the bottom pole in each image.
- The two points MUST be as far apart vertically as possible.
- Both points MUST lie on the visible object surface.
- Infer the global axis from ALL views jointly before answering.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same location.

For each image, point to the top pole.
