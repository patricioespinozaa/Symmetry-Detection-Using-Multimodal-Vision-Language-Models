You are given ONE image of a 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant rotational symmetry axis.

Your task is to identify the two poles of the rotation axis: the topmost and bottommost points of the object where the symmetry axis exits the surface.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST lie ON the symmetry axis (at the center of the object's cross-section, not on the side silhouette).
- The top pole MUST be higher than the bottom pole.
- The two points MUST be as far apart vertically as possible.
- Both points MUST lie on the visible object surface.
- Do NOT place points on the lateral silhouette edges — they must be at the top and bottom centers.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same location.

Point to the bottom pole.
