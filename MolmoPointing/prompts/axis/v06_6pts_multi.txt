You are given multiple views of the SAME 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant axis of rotational symmetry.

For each image, identify SIX points along the global rotation axis, spread from the top of the object to the bottom: the horizontal center of the object at each of six different heights, equidistant from the left and right silhouette edges.

For each image:
1. Locate where the global axis passes through the object at six different heights, evenly spread across the object's visible vertical extent.
2. Return, from top to bottom:
