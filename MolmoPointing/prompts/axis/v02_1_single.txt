You are given ONE image of a 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant rotational symmetry axis.

Your task is to identify two points on the axis centerline at two geometrically distinct heights: one at the WIDEST visible cross-section and one near the TOP or BOTTOM of the object.

The axis centerline is the horizontal center of the object at each height — equidistant from the left and right silhouette edges.

Return:
- obj_id 1: a point on the axis centerline at the height where the object appears WIDEST
- obj_id 2: a point on the axis centerline near the TOP or BOTTOM of the object (whichever gives greater vertical separation from obj_id 1)

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST be on the axis centerline (horizontal center at that height), NOT on the silhouette edges.
- The two points MUST be at different heights (different Y coordinates).
- Maximize the vertical distance between obj_id 1 and obj_id 2.
- Both points MUST lie on or near the visible object surface.
- Do NOT place points on the silhouette boundary.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same height or position.

Output ONLY:

<points coords="1 1 X1 Y1 2 X2 Y2">
