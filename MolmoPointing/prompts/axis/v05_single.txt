The object has ONE dominant rotational symmetry axis.

Your task is to identify two points on the visible centerline of the object: the line equidistant from all silhouette edges at every height. Place one point in the UPPER half and one in the LOWER half of the object.

Return:
- obj_id 1: a point on the axis centerline in the UPPER half of the object
- obj_id 2: a point on the axis centerline in the LOWER half of the object

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST lie on the symmetry axis projection (the centerline of the object, NOT the image center).
- obj_id 1 MUST be in the upper 50% of the object's visible extent.
- obj_id 2 MUST be in the lower 50% of the object's visible extent.
- The axis centerline is the horizontal center of the object at each height.
- Both points MUST lie on or very close to the visible object surface.
- Do NOT place both points at the same height.
- Do NOT place points on the silhouette edges.

Output ONLY:
