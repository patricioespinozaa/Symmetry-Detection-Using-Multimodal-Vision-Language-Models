The object has ONE dominant rotational symmetry axis.

Your task is to identify the axis direction from the object's overall geometry, then place two points on its visible centerline: one in the UPPER half and one in the LOWER half of the object.

The axis centerline is the line through the horizontal center of the object at every height — equidistant from the left and right silhouette edges.

Return:
- obj_id 1: a point on the axis centerline in the UPPER half of the object
- obj_id 2: a point on the axis centerline in the LOWER half of the object

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST lie on the axis centerline (equidistant from both silhouette edges at that height).
- obj_id 1 MUST be in the upper 50% of the object's visible extent.
- obj_id 2 MUST be in the lower 50% of the object's visible extent.
- Both points MUST lie on or very near the visible object surface (not floating in space).
- Do NOT place points on the silhouette edges.
- Do NOT place both points at the same height.
- The axis centerline direction reveals the object's principal orientation — the direction it would be oriented if stood upright.
- If the object is approximately vertical, both points should share approximately the same X coordinate.

Output ONLY:
