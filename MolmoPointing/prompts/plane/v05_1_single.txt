- obj_id 1 MUST have a smaller Y value (higher in the image) than obj_id 2.
- Maximize the VERTICAL distance between the two points specifically (not diagonal distance).
- Both points MUST lie on the visible object surface.
- Use the horizontal center of the object as a guide for where the trace runs.
- Do NOT choose extremal points in arbitrary diagonal directions.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same location.
