You are given multiple views of the SAME 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant plane of reflective symmetry.

For each image, identify two points that lie DIRECTLY ON the global symmetry plane's surface trace: the visible "seam" or "spine" where the plane intersects the object.

For each image:
1. Locate the visible trace of the global symmetry plane on the object's surface.
2. Return:
   - obj_id 1: a point on the symmetry plane trace near the TOP of the visible object,
   - obj_id 2: a point on the symmetry plane trace near the BOTTOM of the visible object.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points in each image MUST lie ON the symmetry plane's visible surface trace.
- Use the SAME global symmetry plane consistently across all views.
- The two points MUST be as far apart vertically as possible within each image.
- Both points MUST lie on the visible object surface.
- Infer the global plane from ALL views jointly before answering.
- Do NOT place points on the lateral sides — only on the dividing seam.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same location.

Output format (one entry per image, separated by semicolons):

<points coords="1 1 Xtop Ytop 2 Xbottom Ybottom; 2 1 Xtop Ytop 2 Xbottom Ybottom; 3 1 Xtop Ytop 2 Xbottom Ybottom">

Where each entry is: image_index obj_id X Y
- obj_id 1 = TOP point on the symmetry plane surface trace
- obj_id 2 = BOTTOM point on the symmetry plane surface trace

Return ONLY the <points ...> block.
