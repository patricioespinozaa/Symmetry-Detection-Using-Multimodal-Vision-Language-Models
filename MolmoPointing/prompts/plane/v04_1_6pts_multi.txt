You are given multiple views of the SAME 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant plane of reflective symmetry.

Step 1: Identify the global symmetry plane orientation from ALL views jointly.
Step 2: For each image, using that global orientation, compute SIX horizontal midpoints of the object's left-right extent, at six different heights spread from the top of the object to the bottom.

These midpoints lie on the symmetry plane's projected trace.

For each image:
1. Project the global symmetry plane's orientation into that view.
2. Return, from top to bottom:
   - obj_id 1: midpoint at the TOP of the object,
   - obj_id 2: midpoint in the upper quarter,
   - obj_id 3: midpoint slightly above the vertical midpoint,
   - obj_id 4: midpoint slightly below the vertical midpoint,
   - obj_id 5: midpoint in the lower quarter,
   - obj_id 6: midpoint at the BOTTOM of the object.

The horizontal midpoint at height Y = (X_left_edge + X_right_edge) / 2 at that height, where "left/right" is defined relative to the global symmetry plane's orientation, not necessarily the image's vertical axis.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- All six points MUST lie on the symmetry plane trace (the center of the object's bilateral extent at that height).
- Use the SAME global symmetry plane consistently across all views.
- The six points MUST be at six clearly different heights, ordered top (obj_id 1) to bottom (obj_id 6), in EVERY image.
- EVERY point MUST be located ON OR WITHIN THE VISIBLE OBJECT ITSELF in that view — never on the background. If the computed midpoint at a given height would fall outside the object in a particular view, place that point on the nearest point of the object's visible surface at that height instead of on empty background.
- Do NOT place points on the silhouette edges — place them at the computed midpoint between the edges.
- Do NOT collapse two or more points to the same height.
- Infer the global plane from ALL views jointly before answering, and verify that the six midpoints in every image are consistent with that SAME global plane.

Output format (one entry per image, separated by semicolons):

<points coords="1 1 X1 Y1 2 X2 Y2 3 X3 Y3 4 X4 Y4 5 X5 Y5 6 X6 Y6; 2 1 X1 Y1 2 X2 Y2 3 X3 Y3 4 X4 Y4 5 X5 Y5 6 X6 Y6; 3 1 X1 Y1 2 X2 Y2 3 X3 Y3 4 X4 Y4 5 X5 Y5 6 X6 Y6">

Where each entry is: image_index obj_id X Y (repeated for obj_id 1 through 6)
- obj_id 1 = TOP midpoint, obj_id 6 = BOTTOM midpoint, obj_id 2-5 = intermediate midpoints top to bottom

Return ONLY the <points ...> block.
