You are given multiple views of the SAME 3D object.

The object has ONE dominant plane of reflective symmetry.

Step 1: Identify the global symmetry plane orientation from ALL views jointly (it is not necessarily aligned with any single image's vertical axis).
Step 2: For each image, using THAT global orientation, compute the horizontal midpoint of the object's left-right extent at two different heights: one near the TOP and one near the BOTTOM.

These midpoints lie on the symmetry plane's projected trace.

For each image:
1. Project the global symmetry plane's orientation into that view.
2. Return:
   - obj_id 1: the midpoint of the left-right extent near the TOP, computed relative to the global plane's orientation,
   - obj_id 2: the midpoint of the left-right extent near the BOTTOM, computed the same way.

The horizontal midpoint at height Y = (X_left_edge + X_right_edge) / 2 at that height, where "left/right" is defined relative to the global symmetry plane's orientation, not necessarily the image's vertical axis.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Both points MUST lie on the symmetry plane trace (the center of the object's bilateral extent at that height).
- Use the SAME global symmetry plane consistently across all views.
- obj_id 1 MUST be in the upper half of the object's visible extent in each image; obj_id 2 in the lower half.
- Both points MUST lie on or within the visible object.
- Do NOT place points on the silhouette edges — place them at the computed midpoint between the edges.
- Do NOT collapse both points to the same height.
- Infer the global plane from ALL views jointly before answering, and verify that the midpoints chosen in every image are consistent with that SAME global plane.

Output format (one entry per image, separated by semicolons):

<points coords="1 1 Xtop Ytop 2 Xbottom Ybottom; 2 1 Xtop Ytop 2 Xbottom Ybottom; 3 1 Xtop Ytop 2 Xbottom Ybottom">

Where each entry is: image_index obj_id X Y
- obj_id 1 = TOP midpoint of the left-right extent (on the symmetry plane trace)
- obj_id 2 = BOTTOM midpoint of the left-right extent (on the symmetry plane trace)

Return ONLY the <points ...> block.
